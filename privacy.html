Mirsaad — Siyaasadda Xogta · Privacy Policy

Taariikhda · Effective: 8 October 2026

Mirsaad waa app madax-bannaan oo kaa caawiya inaad bundle-yo internet iyo hadal ka iibsato agent-yada Mirsaad. Mirsaad ma aha Somtel, Telesom, ZAAD ama E-Dahab, mana matalo.

Mirsaad is an independent app that helps you buy data and voice bundles from Mirsaad agents. Mirsaad is not, and does not represent, Somtel, Telesom, ZAAD or E-Dahab.

1. Xogta aan ururino · Data we collect

  • Profile: magacaaga iyo lambarada Somtel / Telesom ee aad gelisay. Your name and the Somtel / Telesom numbers you enter.
  • Iibsashooyinka · Purchases: bundle-ka, lambarka la shubayo, qiimaha, wallet-ka (ZAAD / E-Dahab), lambarka lacagta laga bixiyay, agent-ka aad dooratay iyo waqtiga. The bundle, recipient number, price, wallet, paying number, chosen agent and time.
  • Aqoonsi qarsoon · Anonymous ID: aqoonsi random ah oo app-ku u sameeyo qalabkaaga si iibsashooyinkaaga loo kala saaro. A random account ID created for your device to keep your purchases apart.
  • Password-ka: waxaa lagu keydiyaa kaliya taleefankaaga isagoo hash ah; nalooma soo diro. Stored only on your phone, hashed; never sent to us.

2. Ogolaanshaha taleefanka · Phone permissions

  • Wac (CALL_PHONE): si app-ku u furo code-ka USSD ee lacag-bixinta ZAAD / E-Dahab markaad adigu riixdo "Bixi". To dial the ZAAD / E-Dahab USSD payment code when you tap Pay.
  • Xaaladda taleefanka (READ_PHONE_STATE): si loo doorto SIM-ka lacagta laga bixinayo. To choose which SIM the payment is sent from.
  • App-ku ma akhriyo SMS-yadaada, wicitaannadaada ama liiska xiriirada. The app does not read your SMS, calls or contacts.

3. Sida aan u isticmaalno · How we use it

Si iibsashadaada loogu diro agent-ka aad dooratay, si uu u xaqiijiyo una shubo bundle-ka, iyo si aad u aragto taariikhdaada iibsashada. Xogtaada lama iibiyo, xayeysiisna looma isticmaalo.

To send your purchase to the agent you chose so they can confirm and deliver it, and to show your purchase history. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

4. Cidda la wadaagto · Who it is shared with

  • Agent-ka aad dooratay iyo maamulka Mirsaad — si iibsashada loo fuliyo. The agent you chose and Mirsaad admins — to fulfil the purchase.
  • Supabase — server-ka xogta lagu keydiyo (adeeg-bixiye). Our database host (service provider).
  • WhatsApp — kaliya markaad adigu riixdo "Caawimaad" ama "La wadaag". Only when you tap Help or Share.

5. Amniga · Security

Xogta waxaa lagu diraa HTTPS (sir ah). Agent kastaa wuxuu arkaa kaliya iibsashooyinka loo soo diray. Data is sent encrypted over HTTPS. Each agent only sees the purchases sent to them.

6. Tirtirka account-ka · Deleting your account

App-ka gudihiisa: Profile → Tirtir account-ka · Delete account. In the app: Profile → Delete account.

App la'aan: u dir WhatsApp +252 63 715 5262 fariin leh "Delete my Mirsaad account" iyo lambarka account-ka. Waxaan tirtiraynaa 30 maalmood gudahood. Without the app: WhatsApp +252 63 715 5262 with "Delete my Mirsaad account" and your account number. We delete it within 30 days.

Waxa la tirtiro: magacaaga, lambaradaada iyo aqoonsiga account-ka. Waxa la hayo: diiwaanka iibsashooyinka (lambarada, qiimaha, waqtiga) si loogu hayo xisaabaadka agent-yada ilaa 3 sano, adiga laguma xirayo. Deleted: your name, numbers and account ID. Kept: purchase records (numbers, amounts, times) for the agents' accounts for up to 3 years, no longer linked to you.

7. Carruurta · Children

Mirsaad waxaa loogu talagalay dadka 18 jirka ka weyn. Mirsaad is meant for people aged 18 and over.

8. Isbeddel iyo xiriir · Changes and contact

Haddii siyaasaddan la beddelo, taariikhda kore ayaa la cusbooneysiinayaa. Su'aalo: WhatsApp +252 63 715 5262.

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. Questions: WhatsApp +252 63 715 5262.
